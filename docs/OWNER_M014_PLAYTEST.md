# Owner checklist — Mission 014 is not accepted yet

You do not need to compile anything. The public download is a synthetic sound
and chart test. The actual Cream build is retained locally until a private
transfer is available; no public Cream link has been created.

Once you have the authorized private ZIP, use current7-Zip on Windows11, enter
the password supplied separately, and extract everything. Open
**Guitar-Boy-Advance-M014-Cream-Private.gba** in mGBA (File → Load ROM), or copy
that exact .gba onto your supported GBA flash cartridge. Do not open the ZIP or
synthetic .gba expecting Cream. The identity should say M014 / CREAM / LOCAL.
Use mGBA's Tools → Settings → Keyboard to check your keyboard's GBA button bindings.

- Confirm left-to-right frets are physical LEFT, B, A, including B+A chords.
- Press START before the first note: music/highway stop; PAUSED is readable.
- Wait, then press START again: music/notes resume together without jumping.
- Hold START: it must not repeatedly pause/resume.
- Pause with guitar audible; then repeat after missing a note mutes the guitar.
- Pause during each type of sustain. Keep its fret down and resume.
- Release a fret while paused, resume without it, then try release/re-press.
- Hold B+A, release only B during pause, and check A continues independently.
- Press a new fret during pause and hold it while resuming: no free note hit.
- Repeat pause/resume rapidly, and try one ten-minute pause.
- Press START+SELECT together and in quick succession both ways: return to selector.
- Press SELECT while paused: exit, no results/replay or accidental restart.
- Finish a song after pauses; check results, replay and return to selector.
- Pause near the last notes; confirm no ending softlock.
- Watch for a highway wobble, missing lines, flashing labels, stale panel or stuck sound.
- Check Original Demo's existing LEFT/RIGHT/A/B controls and INPUT / CHORD TEST.

Please report device/flash cartridge or mGBA version, which step failed, roughly
where in the song, buttons held, and whether the guitar was audible. A short
phone video helps. Emulator tests do not count as your acceptance. Mission013
handheld acceptance and Mission014 acceptance are separate pending decisions.
