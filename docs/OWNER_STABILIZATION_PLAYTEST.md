# Owner playtest — provisional stabilization candidate

No compilation is needed to play a supplied ROM. Obtain the exact candidate
through an authorized private download first; this session currently lacks that
route. No old or synthetic ROM should be mistaken for the real Cream build.

Use 7-Zip on Windows 11 to extract the AES-256 ZIP with the separately supplied
password. Open `Guitar-Boy-Advance-M014-Song-Private.gba` in mGBA or copy it onto
your supported GBA flash cartridge. The native-All candidate contains real Cream
and Easy/Medium/Hard choices. Start with **Easy**: 395 heads and 78 holds. The
legacy `Cream-Private.gba` profile contains Easy only. Record the source commit
and SHA-256 supplied with the package; the historical visible M014 label alone
is not its source identity.

- Confirm LEFT/B/A on Easy and the difficulty Back/confirm controls.
- Repeat fast exit/restart and results replay. Check labels appear correctly.
- Pause before notes, during holds and near the end. Hold START; it must not toggle repeatedly.
- Resume with old/new/released frets; no free head hits or points while paused.
- Hold B+A; release just one during pause. The remaining hold stays independent.
- Repeat audible and muted guitar pauses; misses mute only guitar and hits recover it.
- Check full song, score, sustained bonuses, results and replay; try a ten-minute pause.
- Watch/listen for road displacement, missing tails, stutter, wrong pitch, clicks or shifted music.
- Verify Original Demo and INPUT / CHORD TEST.
- Use the original synthetic multi-difficulty package for Medium/Hard controls,
  UP/both/DOWN alias handoff, chords, spacing and overlapping holds.

Do not expect native Cream Medium/Hard to be completely playable on a normal
D-pad: 11/14 opposing-direction chords remain unchanged and need an owner policy
decision. Their emulator completion is not physical acceptance. No adapted chart
is included. Scoring reference 15,815 applies to its exact input trace; fractional
hit timing can produce other all-Perfect hold bonuses under the existing rule.

Report GBA model, flash cartridge, build SHA, difficulty, song time, buttons held
and whether guitar was muted. A short phone video helps. The emulator audio
comparison issue remains open. Acceptance, adaptation approval and integration
approval are separate owner decisions; none is inferred from emulator tests.
