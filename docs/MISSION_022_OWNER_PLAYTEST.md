# Mission 022 owner playtest

Open the newly delivered M022 collection in mGBA, or copy it onto an appropriate
GBA flash cartridge. Existing M021 ROMs remain available as fallbacks.

START opens Main; A selects Play. UP/DOWN choose a song. A opens its actual
difficulties; A launches. Synth Demo offers Easy/Medium/Hard. Synth Demo 2 offers
Easy only. Both songs have distinct audio and notes.

During play START pauses. Resume, Restart and Quit act on the current song.
Results offers Retry, Change Difficulty, Song Select and Main Menu.
Try A -> B -> A and B -> A -> B without rebooting, including after Pause/Quit.
Confirm the displayed song, heard music, difficulty and Results remain consistent.

Record ROM filename/checksum, emulator/device/version/configuration, tested
difficulties, transitions, sustained chords, responsiveness and any glitch.
Do not infer original Nintendo GBA certification from handheld emulator testing.
The inherited strict ordinary-output limitation still blocks formal release.

Owner-local private builds and recordings are not authorized for public sharing.
No owner playtest result for M022 is assumed by this checklist.
