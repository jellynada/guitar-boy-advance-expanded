# Owner Arcade75 playtest

No local compilation is needed for a supplied ROM. The real Cream archive must
come through an authorized private transfer; public GitHub plus encryption is
not permission to distribute the recording. A public synthetic review is a
separate original exercise, never a replacement Cream download.

On Windows, use 7-Zip to extract the AES-256 private ZIP with its separately
provided password. Open the enclosed `.gba` in mGBA, or copy it to your supported
GBA flash cartridge. Confirm the source SHA/hash and visible **ARCADE75 / HANDHELD**
song title. The old full-length regression ROM is separately labelled in delivery.

- Easy: LEFT/B/A; Medium: LEFT/RIGHT/B/A; Hard: LEFT/UP-or-DOWN/RIGHT/B/A.
- Try every difficulty; verify the seven formerly opposing Cream combinations.
- Hard: hold UP, add DOWN, release UP, then release DOWN. No duplicate head or early hold release.
- Try playable LEFT+B+A triples, overlapping holds and rapid attacks.
- Miss notes and recover: only guitar mutes; backing continues.
- Pause/resume while audible/muted, during a hold and just before the 75s ending.
- Hold START: it toggles once. No progress/points during pause.
- Check end fade, completed results, prepared note totals and replay/rapid exit.
- Listen for clicks, pitch changes, drift or stutter; watch for road displacement.
- Check INPUT / CHORD TEST and unchanged Original Demo.

Report GBA model, flash cartridge, source SHA, difficulty, song time and held keys.
A short phone video is useful. Diagonal D-pad comfort, actual audio synchronization
and real-GBA acceptance remain owner gates. Emulator results do not accept them.
